## Slide 15
- ENGINEERING / THE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION COMPANY
- Architectural and Infrastructural Solution
- © Engineering Group | 15
- Security enforcement | How access is applied on every request
- At the door
- PingFederate login with MFA for HQ; the token carries brand, market, role and dealer entity; Akamai WAF, bot management and rate limits sit in front of the portal.
- In the BFF and widgets
- Every call goes through API Connect and the BFF: the user's token is propagated, entitlements are checked server-side by brand, market, role and entity; widgets never hold credentials.
- In content and AI
- AEM targeting and repository rules decide visibility; Bedrock retrieval is filtered by the user's permissions before generation, with the verified identity passed by the BFF; only approved models through the AI Gateway; questions and answers logged.
- Evidence
- SonarQube, GitHub Advanced Security and Checkmarx gates in every pipeline; audit log of access and changes; penetration tests by Stellantis Security with findings fixed within agreed SLAs.
- The portal never grants more than the source application: data held in an index or a cache carries the same permission tags, legacy applications keep their own authorisation behind the proxy, and every denial is logged.
